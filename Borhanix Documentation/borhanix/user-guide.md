# User Guide

Welcome to **Borhanix** — this guide walks through how to use the platform as a Student, Instructor, or Admin.

## Getting Started
1. Go to the Borhanix home page.
2. Click **Register** to create an account, or **Login** if you already have one.
3. New accounts are assigned the **Student** role by default. Instructor/Admin access is granted by an administrator.

## For Students

### Browsing & Enrolling in Courses
1. Open the **Course Catalog** from the navigation menu.
2. Click into a course to view its **Course Details** (description, instructor, content outline).
3. Click **Enroll** to request access. Depending on the course settings, enrollment may require approval from an admin/instructor.

### Learning
1. Once enrolled, open the course from your **Student Dashboard**.
2. Use the **Learn** page to move through lessons in order.
3. Your progress is tracked automatically as you complete each lesson.

### Quizzes & Assignments
1. Quizzes and assignments appear inline with course content.
2. Complete a quiz and submit it for immediate or instructor-reviewed scoring.
3. For assignments, upload/submit your work by the due date; your instructor will grade it.

### Attendance (QR)
1. Open **My Card** to display your personal QR attendance code.
2. At an in-person/live session, have your instructor scan your code with the **Attendance Scanner**.
3. Your attendance is recorded automatically once scanned.

### Certificates
1. Once you complete all required content in a course, a **Certificate** becomes available.
2. View or download it from your dashboard or the course's certificate page.

### AI Assistant
1. Open the **AI Assistant** chat from within the platform.
2. Ask questions related to your coursework — the assistant (powered by DeepSeek) will respond in the chat window.

## For Instructors

### Creating a Course
1. Go to the **Instructor Studio**.
2. Click **Create Course** and fill in the title, description, category, and cover image.
3. Save as a draft or publish once ready.

### Managing Content
1. Open **Manage Content** for a course.
2. Use the accordion sidebar to add, reorder, and edit lessons.
3. Attach quizzes/assignments to specific lessons where relevant.

### Duplicating a Course
1. From the course list, select **Duplicate Course**.
2. This creates a deep copy of the course, including its content structure, which you can then modify independently.

### Grading
1. Open the **Grading** view to see pending student submissions.
2. Review each submission and enter a score/feedback.

### Attendance
1. Open the **Attendance Scanner** at the start of a live session.
2. Scan each student's QR code (from their **Student ID Card**) to record attendance in real time.

## For Admins

### Admin Dashboard
The Admin Dashboard gives a platform-wide overview: user counts, course counts, pending enrollments, etc.

### Managing Users
1. Open **User Control**.
2. Search/filter users, update roles (Student/Instructor/Admin), and manage account status.

### Approving Enrollments
1. Open **Enrollment Approvals**.
2. Review pending enrollment requests and approve or reject each one.

## Appearance
- Toggle **Dark Mode** from the navigation/settings menu at any time; your preference applies across all pages.
- Toast notifications will confirm successful actions (e.g., "Enrollment successful") or flag errors.

## Getting Help
If you run into an issue while using Borhanix, contact your platform administrator or open an issue on the project's GitHub repository: https://github.com/kh1aled/borhanix/issues
